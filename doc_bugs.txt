Website Review: Feedback and Change Requests
vmls.edu.in – Vinayaka Mission’s Law School
Prepared by Jero Prince and the Media and Communications Committee (MCC) team for the web development team • 21 September 2026
This report lists the changes requested after a review of the website. It contains 30 numbered requests, grouped by area of the site, and 34 screenshots. Each request gives the page concerned, the issue observed where one was noted, and the change requested. Screenshots marked as supplementary were sent separately from the original review file.
